Also observed: the machine has not been rebooted since the 16:04 crash (`PendingFileRenameOperations` set), so the post-Cisco reboot is still outstanding. Six other driver updates queued (Realtek, HP keyboard/software component, Cirrus Logic, Logitech) are one-shot, not part of the loop.
